• All applicants must take the FEAT (Forman Entrance Admission Test) Accuplacer conducted by FCCU, which tests English and Mathematics skills. In addition to FEAT:• Bachelor of Studies in Biotechnology requires a subject-specific test.• Bachelor of Studies in Computer Science requires two additional Mathematics tests (Quantitative Reasoning, Algebra and Statistics, and Advanced Algebra and Functions).• Students applying for Bachelor of Studies in Biotechnology must have studied Biology in Intermediate or in any other equivalent qualification (A Level, High School, or IB).• Requirements for applying for Bachelor of Studies in Computer Science:• Studied Mathematics in Intermediate or in any other equivalent qualification. Students with a pre-medical (Intermediate) or an equivalent background are also eligible to apply. If they secure admission, they must pass two Mathematics deficiency courses of 6 credit hours within the first year of their Bachelor’s studies.• Minimum 50% marks are required in Intermediate or in any other equivalent qualification.• Admission is based on previous academic performance, FEAT result, and additional tests (if applicable).• Copies of Inter Board Coordination Commission (IBCC) Equivalence Certificate(s) must be submitted by the students who are from O/A Level, High School, or IB backgrounds.• Admission requirements are the same (as mentioned above) for students transferring from other universities to FCCU.
•	Students who have passed their Intermediate Part I and will be taking Part 2 exams in May/June, are allowed to apply on a conditional basis. If they secure admission, it will be a conditional/provisional offer. Admission will be confirmed when we receive the complete Intermediate result within the allotted time frame. (Minimum 50% marks are required for Bachelor of Studies in Computer Science.)
•	Students who have passed 8 O Level subjects (Equivalence from IBCC required) and will be taking A Level papers in summer, will be allowed to apply on a conditional basis. If they secure admission, it will be a conditional/provisional offer. Admission will be confirmed when we receive a copy of the A Level result (within the allotted time frame) showing that the student has passed at least 3 A Level exams (No AS accepted). A Level Equivalence from IBCC will be required as soon as it becomes available. (Minimum 50% marks are required for Bachelor of Studies in Computer Science.)
•	All documents submitted must be attested by a Gazetted Officer.
Students who have completed Intermediate, A Level, High School, IB, or DAE (from a recognized Technical Board) are eligible to apply.
